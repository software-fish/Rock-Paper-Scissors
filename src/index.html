Welcome to a game of Rock, Paper, Scissors

In this game you will be facing the computer in a game of Rock Paper and Scissors.

Basically Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beat Paper and Paper beats Rock

If your option beats the computers option you win the game

Fun fact: In 2006, Christie’s and Sotheby’s—the world’s two largest auction houses—competed for the right to sell an $18 million collection of Impressionist art, including works by Cezanne, Picasso, and Gauguin. Instead of choosing based on sales pitches, the owner, Japanese electronics executive Takashi Hashiyama, told both houses to play a single game of Rock, Paper, Scissors to decide who got the account. Christie went with scissors and won.
